# nrl-2027-player-movements

The NRL's confirmed 2027 signings, departures and retirements from Zero Tackle's
[player movement tracker](https://www.zerotackle.com/nrl/player-movements/), applied to the club squads. It also
cleans up players who sat on two club rosters at once.

The full list of what changed is in [movements_report.md](movements_report.md), regenerated by every build.

## Rules

- **Gains** join the club. **Losses** leave it and join their new club when it's in the game (Super League,
  Championship, QLD Cup, and the Perth Bears via [perth-bears](../perth-bears/)). Otherwise they become free
  agents. A "released" player only leaves the club that released him, because the 2026 database often already
  has him somewhere else.
- **Retired** players are taken off every roster, rep sides included, so they become free agents.
- **One club per player** (rep and international sides excepted). A player stays at the club a movement put him
  at. Otherwise he stays at the highest tier: NRL, then Super League / Championship / League 1, then QLD Cup, then
  NSW Cup, then stale clubs. Stock has about 110 such duplicates, mostly the NSW Cup sides, which still carry 2025
  squads.
- **Short squads** (under 26 men) are topped up with unsigned players. Ex-players from the club's own 2025 record
  go first, then any unsigned man.
- **Lineups** (LeagueMatch, NinesMatch, NRL2026Match) keep every slot. A slot whose player left takes the
  best-rated squad member with that slot's usual role (the role most NRL clubs' players there have), then a
  secondary-role match, then the best rated. A captain, goal kicker or playmaker who left is replaced from the
  halves, or by the best-rated starter.
- **Career mode uses the hidden "2025" club records.** Career data's `career_leaguestructure` lists ids 1-17 for
  the NRL, 20-31 for Super League, and so on. So every hidden 2025 men's club takes its 2026 club's final squad
  and lineups (matched by club icon), and career starts with the same squads as Quick Match.

Players not in the game's database are skipped and listed in the report (2026-11-01: Te Hurinui Twidle, Jasais
Ah Kee, Asher Chapman, all heading to Perth).

## Build

```powershell
python mods\nrl-2027-player-movements\generate.py fetch     # optional: refresh resources\player-movements\
python mods\nrl-2027-player-movements\generate.py build     # overrides + report + mods\perth-bears\squad.json
python mods\perth-bears\generate.py                         # Perth's record uses squad.json
```

Then rebuild **licenseddata** from a stock extraction (INSTRUCTIONS.md 1a). The overrides use the
`"rosterList"` / `"lineupTables"` club keys in [tools/data_mods.py](../../tools/data_mods.py). They resize
records, so they go through `bigant_db`. The generator reads the stock clubs and players from
`_build\stock\licenseddata`.

**Untested in-game.**
