# crowd-team-kits

Source: [mods/crowd-team-kits](../../../mods/crowd-team-kits/) - [README](../../../mods/crowd-team-kits/README.md)

Dresses the crowd in the jerseys and merch of the two teams that are playing, for every club, including the 2026 clubs. Before this mod, the 2026 clubs' fans wore generic clothing.
