# ai-line-break-finishing

Source: [mods/ai-line-break-finishing](../../../mods/ai-line-break-finishing/) - [README](../../../mods/ai-line-break-finishing/README.md)

When the AI makes a clean line break, the runner no longer always runs round to the posts. In 10 % of breaks he still goes to the posts, and if nobody can catch him he showboats (taunts) on the way in.
