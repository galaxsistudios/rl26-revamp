# hd-nrl-club-logos

Source: [mods/hd-nrl-club-logos](../../../mods/hd-nrl-club-logos/) - [README](../../../mods/hd-nrl-club-logos/README.md)

Replaces 95 club emblems with HD renders of the current nrl.com vector badges (`resources/nrl-site-logos/<comp>/<team>/`, pulled by `tools/nrl_site_logos.py`) and, for British clubs, Gallery of League's ~1000px PNGs (`resources/gallery-of-league-logos/<section>/<club>/badge.png`, pulled by `tools/gol_logos.py`). Layouts come from `tools/club_icons.py`, but the stock entries are **replaced**, not appended.
