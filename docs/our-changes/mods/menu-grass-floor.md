# menu-grass-floor

Source: [mods/menu-grass-floor](../../../mods/menu-grass-floor/) - [README](../../../mods/menu-grass-floor/README.md)

Turns the menu floor into grass. The player on the home screen (and on player edit, team select and the other front-end screens that stand a model on the floor) now stands on a dark-lit strip of pitch with mowing stripes running away from the camera, instead of grey concrete.

## Texture replacements: 1

- `textures/14e25d07e8204022_floor_image.dds`

Generators, installers and exe patchers the mod runs itself (see its README):
