# gf-tunnel-team-colour-lights

Source: [mods/gf-tunnel-team-colour-lights](../../../mods/gf-tunnel-team-colour-lights/) - [README](../../../mods/gf-tunnel-team-colour-lights/README.md)

The coloured lights in the Grand Final walkout tunnel take the club colours of the side walking out, instead of the stock lime green. Odd-numbered lights use the primary colour and even-numbered ones use the secondary, so the floor lights form alternating bands down the tunnel and the four uplights on the players go primary / secondary / primary / secondary.
