# headgear-team-colours

Source: [mods/headgear-team-colours](../../../mods/headgear-team-colours/) - [README](../../../mods/headgear-team-colours/README.md)

Kalyn Ponga's headgear always shows Queensland maroon, whoever he's playing for. Headgear colours are fixed per player (`headwearColour1-4` in his player record: maroon base, white pattern), and the game has no "use team colours" switch for headgear. The cricket-era `helmetUseTeam` option is never read in RL26.
