# crowd-fangear

Source: [mods/crowd-fangear](../../../mods/crowd-fangear/) - [README](../../../mods/crowd-fangear/README.md)

Gives every club's fans more merchandise than the one polo, cap and scarf the game ships: fan tees, polos and scarves in the club's colours, carrying its current and classic logos and its wordmark. A **Fangear Library** in the club's Uniforms list shows what a club's crowd wears and turns each design on or off.
