# unlock-all-kits

Source: [mods/unlock-all-kits](../../../mods/unlock-all-kits/) - [README](../../../mods/unlock-all-kits/README.md)

Makes every kit (jersey) in the club data selectable, under its real name. On the stock game 64 kits across 41 clubs are hidden even though their data and textures ship with the game.

## Programs

Generators, installers and exe patchers the mod runs itself (see its README):

- [launch.py](../../../mods/unlock-all-kits/launch.py)
