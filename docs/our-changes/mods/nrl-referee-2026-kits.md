# nrl-referee-2026-kits

Source: [mods/nrl-referee-2026-kits](../../../mods/nrl-referee-2026-kits/) - [README](../../../mods/nrl-referee-2026-kits/README.md)

Adds the 2026 NRL referee jerseys as three **new** referee kits: **NRL 2026 Orange**, **NRL 2026 Yellow** and **NRL 2026 Blue**. The stock referee kits (NRL 2025 green, orange and white, and the others) stay as they are.
