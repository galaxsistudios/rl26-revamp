# nrl-led-boards

Source: [mods/nrl-led-boards](../../../mods/nrl-led-boards/) - [README](../../../mods/nrl-led-boards/README.md)

The stadium LED ribbons (field-level ring and stand fascia) show the **home club's** board at any ground that has LED panels. A board is the team's 4 sponsors, then its competition's 4, then the mod's 2 (RL26 Revamp, Galaxsi Studios).
